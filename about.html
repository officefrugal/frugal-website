Our Story

Built on a Simple Belief

Frugal Consultancy Services Pvt Ltd was incorporated in 2022 with a clear mandate: give growing businesses access to the kind of technology and marketing expertise that was previously only available to large enterprises with big budgets.

We saw too many small and mid-sized businesses either overpaying for bloated agency retainers or making do with junior talent that cost less but delivered less. We knew there was a better way.

By keeping our team lean, our processes efficient, and our focus sharp, we consistently deliver at 30–40% below market rates — without ever compromising on seniority or quality.

Today we serve clients across technology, retail, education, healthcare, real estate, and fintech — both in India and internationally.
